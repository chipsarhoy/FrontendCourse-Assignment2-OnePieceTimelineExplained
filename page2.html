Summary

Entering the Grand Line are Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp, and Sanji on their ship the Going Merry. After gathering supplies from Loguetown in the East Blue, they traverse up Reverse Mountain. The gateway that connects the East Blue to the Grand Line, here in the Grand Line, the crew is destined to meet the next 4 essential members that join the Straw Hat Pirates. They members are discovered to be the Doctor, Tony Tony Chopper; the Archaeologist, Nico Robin; the Shipwright, Franky; and the Musician, Brook. They know that at the end of the Grand Line, within the New World, lies the One Piece at the last island. By passing through the first half of the Grand Line, they become stronger, gain more allies, and learn exactly how treacherous their journey is yet to become.
